Chapter one

Where it began

Techzoo was founded in New York in 2015.

It is a proudly American brand, and it began with a passion for purposeful organization: a closet where every piece has its place and nothing is harmed on the way there.

We make one thing, the velvet hanger, in adult, kids and baby sizes. Techzoo describes itself as one of the oldest and most loved velvet hanger brands, and more than a million customers have made a home for it.
